01 / Portfolio overview

Five brands. One platform.

The brand guide for Kasu's direct booking portfolio: who each brand is, what it looks like, how it speaks, and the rules that hold for all of them.

The platform

Kasu sits beneath every brand.

Kasu is the operating and trust platform behind the five public brands. It owns inventory, booking, payments, services, customer data, attribution, and guest support. Public positioning always stays brand-specific.

One house can appear on several brands when it legitimately qualifies. It keeps one calendar and one central price book.

Use these verbatim.

The portfolio

Five brands, five visual systems.

Not five recolors of one page. Choose a brand to re-skin this guide in its tokens.

Principles

Rules that hold everywhere.

  1. Tokens, not hard-coding

    Brand styling comes from tokens or the brand's scoped layer. Shared components never hold a brand color.

  2. Always endorsed

    Every customer-facing sub-brand lockup carries by Kasu beneath the sub-brand, using the official vector.

  3. Only verified claims

    Capacity, bedrooms, amenities, courts, gyms, rates, and reviews must map to a live listing with dated evidence.

  4. Concept is not proof

    Concept or AI imagery can guide design. It is replaced with authorized property or guest media before a page publishes.

  5. Original execution

    Familiar patterns are welcome. Other companies' assets, wording, layout measurements, and trade dress are not.

  6. Consent is per brand

    Never infer consent for one brand from activity on another.

03 / Lockups & endorsement

Every sub-brand is by Kasu.

The full endorsed lockup, the sub-brand with a subordinate by Kasu line, appears wherever a guest meets the brand.

Current brand ·

Approved marks.

Where it's required

The full lockup goes on.

  • Website mastheads, mobile navigation, and footers
  • Property, availability, checkout, and confirmation surfaces
  • Guest communications
  • Social profiles, share cards, and campaigns
  • Collateral and signage
  • Merchandise

A monogram may appear alone only in constrained formats such as a favicon or app icon. The nearest brand-identifying surface must then show the full endorsed lockup.

Protect the mark

Rules for every lockup.

check_circle Do

  • Use approved vector files only.
  • Use the official Kasu wordmark for the endorsement line.
  • Write the endorsement as by Kasu.

cancel Don't

  • Retype a wordmark in HTML as a substitute.
  • Stretch, recolor outside approved variants, or add shadows, outlines, or effects.
  • Rearrange the endorsement, or write by Kasu Resorts.
  • Use monogram text fallbacks (PH, KR, O, SH) anywhere but empty states.

04 / Voice & tone

Plain. Specific. Verifiable.

Say exactly what the house, court, or gym offers, and only what has been verified. Short declarative sentences. Sentence case. No hype, no fake urgency.

Principles

How every brand speaks.

  1. Speak to the organizer

    The person planning the trip is the hero. Relieve their workload: one clear brief, one plan, one team.

  2. Group-first

    Everyone, together, under one roof, on your group's schedule.

  3. Calm confidence

    Short declarative sentences. Sentence case. No hype and no fake urgency.

  4. Transparent pricing

    Give total-price context. Never hide mandatory fees.

  5. Brand vocabulary

    Use each brand's organizer term and verified label exactly as configured.

  6. One voice per surface

    Never blend brand voices. Messaging templates inherit the booking's brand voice.

Avoid

Language that doesn't belong.

  • Claims about amenities, equipment, capacity, or experiences that aren't verified for that specific property.
  • Wording copied from competitors or listing marketplaces.
  • Hype words, stacked exclamation marks, and all-caps shouting in body copy. Uppercase is reserved for kickers and specific display treatments.
  • A generic Book now as the only call to action. Pair it with organizer and planning actions.

System & error copy

Say what happened, then what to do.

Keep unsaved, saved, background sync, and integration-failure states distinct, each with a plain-language recovery action.

07 / Foundations

Calm under pressure.

Fresh, energetic, and unmistakably hospitality-led. Brand tokens differ, but spacing, accessibility, interaction, and base components are shared.

Product feel

Five ideas behind every screen.

  1. Clarity, original execution

    Familiar mental models: list and detail editing, calendar-first pricing, obvious publish state, guest-total previews.

  2. Tactile

    Large hit targets, smooth transitions, clear pressed and focus states, layered surfaces, immediate save feedback.

  3. Progressive disclosure

    Daily work stays simple. Advanced detail opens only on request.

  4. Fast enough for operations

    Motion never delays an action. Save, sync, availability freshness, and errors stay visible.

  5. One coherent product

    Tokens differ by brand. Everything else is shared.

08 / Accessibility

Readable by everyone.

WCAG AA is the floor for every brand, every surface, every control.

Requirements

Non-negotiable.

  1. AA contrast

    Check every brand pairing. Translucent and blurred surfaces need an opaque fallback that still passes.

  2. Visible focus

    Keyboard focus and logical tab order everywhere, including date pickers, galleries, side sheets, and the inbox.

  3. 44px targets

    Every touch target is at least 44 by 44 pixels.

  4. Reduced motion

    prefers-reduced-motion is respected through the global reduction rule.

  5. More than color

    Essential meaning never lives only in color, transparency, blur, or animation.

11 / Checklist

Before anything ships.

Run through this list before merging any storefront or guest-surface change.

Pre-merge

Every item, every time.

  • Brand styling comes from tokens or the brand's scoped layer. No brand hex in shared components.
  • Every customer-facing lockup shows the official by Kasu endorsement.
  • Copy uses the brand's configured organizer term, verified label, and signature.
  • Every public claim maps to verified listing data. No concept imagery on public pages.
  • Concept brands stay behind their release switch until cleared.
  • No competitor wording, assets, or trade dress copied.
  • Interaction motion is 160–240ms, nothing blocks an action, and reduced motion is respected.
  • AA contrast, visible focus, 44px targets, and opaque fallbacks for glass.
  • Any conflict between sources is surfaced, not resolved silently.
